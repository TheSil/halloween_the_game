Tip: finishers

A finisher (an execution without a grab) always counts as a Stealth Kill. On a victim at Stalk 3 with Michael at Bloodthirst 3 it is a Perfect Kill as well. Those are the two biggest multipliers (Perfect 1.25, Stealth 1.15, together 1.438), so a finisher is the easiest way to the most points per kill.

Kill modifiers

Multipliers apply to the points of the kill and multiply with each other.
Any killslash kills and executions
Perfect Kill
×1.25
The victim is at Stalk 3, Michael is at Bloodthirst 3, and the kill also qualifies as a Stealth Kill. A Perfect Kill therefore always comes together with Stealth Kill.
Stealth Kill
×1.15
The victim is not aware of Michael at the moment of death.
  • Residents, special targets and police (all computer-controlled) become aware after Michael has been in their sight for 2 seconds, and stay aware for 15 seconds after losing sight of him.
  • Civilians (players) are aware only while Michael is in their view right now. Looking away at the moment of death counts as unaware.
  • Executions: a finisher (no grab) always counts as unaware. A grab execution counts as unaware if the victim had not noticed Michael before the grab.
Terrified Kill
×1.05
The victim is in the terrified state (fear meter full) at the moment of death.
Wounded Kill
×1.05
The victim was at low health before the killing blow: civilians at 25% health or less, residents, special targets and police at 30% or less. What counts is the health before the killing blow, so the blow itself does not have to be weak.
Lights Out Kill
×1.025
The victim was caught in a blackout caused by Michael. It stays on that victim afterwards.
  • Power box: the victim was inside the power box’s area at the moment Michael switched it off.
  • Blackout ability: the victim was hit by Michael’s Blackout.
Slash kill onlynever on executions
Ranged Kill
×1.15
The killing damage came from a thrown weapon.
Bleed Out Kill
×1.10
The victim is bleeding at the moment of death.
One Hit Kill
×1.10
Michael had damaged this victim exactly once before the killing blow. In practice that is a kill on the second hit.
